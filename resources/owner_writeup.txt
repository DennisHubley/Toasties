Hi, I'm Brett, owner and operator of Toasties. I've been working in the food industry for over 20 years, and I've always had a passion for creating food that brings people together. Growing up on Nova Scotia's South Shore, I've developed a deep appreciation for our local communities and the incredible ingredients produced here. That's why I love featuring locally sourced products whenever possible, especially in our rotating Toastie of the Day specials.

At Toasties, every grilled cheese is made fresh to order on classic white bread grilled in our signature garlic parmesan butter and filled with quality cheeses and carefully selected ingredients. Whether you stop by at a farmers' market, beach, festival, or community event, my goal is to serve comforting, flavour-packed food with a friendly smile and a little creativity.

Thanks for supporting local—I look forward to serving you a Toastie soon!
